### Abstract
Let *X* = {*X*(*t*), *t* ∈ [0, T]} be a second-order random process for which *n* independent realizations are observed on a fixed grid of *p* time points. Under mild regularity assumptions, we show the asymptotic normality of suitable nonparametric estimators of the trend function μ = E*X* in the space of continuous functions as *n* and *p* increase, and derive approximate simultaneous confidence bands for μ.
