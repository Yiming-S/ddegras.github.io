Courses taught regularly in the Department of Mathematics at the University of
Massachusetts Boston. Consult the official catalog links for current sections
and schedules.

## [MATH 545 — Probability & Statistics I for Secondary Teachers](https://courses.umb.edu/course_catalog/course_info/grd_math_all_545)

Probability models and statistical foundations for secondary-teacher
preparation, including discrete and continuous random variables, expectation,
variance, common probability distributions, and introductory statistical
analysis.

## [MATH 648 — Computational Statistics](https://courses.umb.edu/course_catalog/course_info/grd_math_all_648)

Statistical inference through mathematical foundations, case studies, and
computation. Topics include estimation, confidence intervals, hypothesis
testing, ANOVA, regression, nonparametric methods, and analysis of real-world
data using R, MATLAB, or Python.

*Prerequisite: MATH 647 or permission of the instructor.*
