My research develops statistical methods for structured, high-dimensional, and functional data.

## Online and functional methods

The [`onlinePCA`](https://github.com/ddegras/onlinePCA) project develops algorithms for online principal component analysis, including recursive updates of means, covariances, and principal directions.

## Segmentation and sparse estimation

The [`sparseGFL`](https://github.com/ddegras/sparseGFL) project studies sparse group fused lasso methods for joint model segmentation and sparse estimation in high-dimensional piecewise regression models.

## Switching and multiblock models

The [`switch-ssm`](https://github.com/ddegras/switch-ssm) and [`tensorMCCA`](https://github.com/ddegras/tensorMCCA) projects address Markov-switching state-space models and tensor-based multiple canonical correlation analysis.

## Functional inference and neuroimaging

Related work includes simultaneous confidence bands for functional data, statistical computing, convex and combinatorial optimization, and applications in neuroimaging.
