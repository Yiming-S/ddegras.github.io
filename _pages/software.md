### [onlinePCA](https://github.com/ddegras/onlinePCA)
Algorithms for online principal component analysis and recursive updates of sample means and covariances.


### [sparseGFL](https://github.com/ddegras/sparseGFL)
Sparse group fused lasso methods for model segmentation and sparse estimation in high-dimensional piecewise regression models.

### [switch-ssm](https://github.com/ddegras/switch-ssm)
MATLAB implementations for Markov-switching state-space models.

### [tensorMCCA](https://github.com/ddegras/tensorMCCA)
Tensor-based multiple canonical correlation analysis for multiblock data.

### [SCBmeanfd](https://github.com/ddegras/SCBmeanfd)
Simultaneous confidence bands and related inference tools for functional data.
